## Overview
Magic is complicated. it can be different across systems depending on the local shape of the Magical Planes.
## Aether
The Aether here is quite strong, due to the proximity of "Mother Tal", which has quite a large presence in the magical planes.
## Magical Planes
The Magical planes are hidden dimentions of energy that are tied to the physical plane by something called Aether.
